Privacy Policy

What I collect

When you buy or contact me, I receive the information you provide, such as your name, email address, and order details. Card details are handled by the payment processor (for example Stripe or Gumroad) and are not stored by me.

How it is used

To deliver products, provide support, process refunds, send receipts and important product updates, and meet legal and tax obligations.

Sharing

I do not sell your personal information. It is shared only with service providers needed to run the business (payment, hosting, and email providers) or when required by law.

This website

This site uses no analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies of its own. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security.

Software products

If a software product stores data you enter, that data is used only to provide the service to you. Details are in the product's documentation.

Retention and your choices

Order records are kept as long as needed for legal and tax purposes. You can ask to see, correct, or delete your information by emailing dwhitaker.work@gmail.com.

Changes
